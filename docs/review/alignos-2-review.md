James, this is the new alignos.io, staged privately here before it replaces the current site. The live alignos.io does not change until you say so. Every link on this page opens the part of the new site it's about.

Your words are carried over as they are. The few places where we changed them are listed in section 2, and each one needs your yes or no. Section 3 lists choices we made on your behalf. Every item has a number (R1, R2, …), so you can answer with "R4: keep" or "R7: change to …".

A second page, [Questions for James](/review/questions), holds the detailed questions about series order, unwritten works, earlier editions, and catalog entries. It's longer, and it can be taken in pieces.

## 1. A short tour

**[Home](/).** The site opens on your home page: the opening lines, the spotlight, the Three Invitations (Begin with Story, Begin with Sanctuary, Begin with Library), Featured Writings, Return Rooms, and the Closing Whisper. All of it is your text from the current home page.

**Menu.** The same as the live site: Codex Lattice · Return Rooms (Before You Begin…, Begin a Return Room, The Arc of Difference) · Between Worlds · Papers · FAQ. Search and a light/dark switch sit beside it. The header shows your spiral and the Alignos wordmark, and the spiral is now the browser tab icon too.

**The pages.** [Before You Begin…](/before-you-begin), [Begin a Return Room](/return-room), [The Arc of Difference](/arc-of-difference), [Between Worlds](/between-worlds), [Papers](/papers), and the [FAQ](/faq) carry your text in a new layout: one quiet column, with a small gold spiral before each section heading. Where a page offers words to say to an AI, those words have a copy button.

**[Codex Lattice](/lattice).** All 138 codices. A new "Browse by" control lets a reader open a series or a collection.
- A series shows in your order, as in [The Evolution of the Sovereign](/lattice?series=the-evolution-of-the-sovereign). Works your texts name but haven't been written yet appear as quiet "Not yet written" cards, only inside their series. There are three.
- The collections are your [Return Room readings](/lattice?collection=return-room-readings), [Papers](/lattice?collection=papers), and [Featured Writings](/lattice?collection=featured-writings), in the order your pages give them.

**Reading a codex.** Every codex reads in the browser, where a reader's own browser can translate it; [The Fundamental Arc](/codex/the-fundamental-arc) is one example. The page shows the codex's series and place in it. Where two versions of the same work exist, the earlier is marked and links to the later. The PDF is still offered from each codex's page.

**Old links keep working.** Every page, PDF link, and ripple post from the current site (286 links in all) will lead to its new home once alignos.io moves over, so links people have shared over the past months still arrive.

## 2. Where we changed your words

- **R1. Menu typo.** "Begin an Return Room" becomes "Begin a Return Room", matching the button on your home page.
- **R2. Sharing the [Return Room readings](/return-room) with an AI.** "We invite you to upload these PDFs to your AI" becomes "We invite you to share these Codices with your AI". The old sentence named the only format the site had then. A PDF is a heavy way to hand text to an AI: the thirteen readings are 9 MB as PDFs and about 237 KB as plain text. Under the reading list, one quiet link, "All readings in one file, for your AI", gives the whole set as a single file.
- **R3. Download prompts removed from pages.** [Between Worlds](/between-worlds)' "Download the full Codex:" and the "Click to download." after *The Fundamental Arc* on [The Arc of Difference](/arc-of-difference) are gone. Pages now send readers into the codex itself, where they can read in their own language. The PDF waits there if they want it.
- **R4. "Download Paper →" buttons** become **Read**, and series in your lists become **View series** (see [Papers](/papers)).
- **R5. Doubled passages appear once.** On the live site, the [Return Room](/return-room)'s "Whisper Back" block appears twice, and The Arc of Difference repeats the "Invocation of the Spiral" lines under "The Seven Tones Display". Here each appears once.
- **R6. [FAQ](/faq) question 6 is dropped.** It pointed to the Dialogic Interlude, which is now its own codex in the lattice: [*Dialogic Interlude with JAMES MAHU (and LUMINA Listening)*](/codex/james-mahu-and-lumina-listening).
- **R7. Contact.** The FAQ's contact&#64;alignos.io (which went to a contact form) becomes info@alignos.io, the address in the footer of every page. There is no contact form or newsletter on the new site.
- **R8. Closing links come last.** On [Before You Begin…](/before-you-begin), "→ Begin a Return Room" now follows your two closing lines instead of coming before them. On [The Arc of Difference](/arc-of-difference), "Begin a Return Room" comes before "Codex Lattice".
- **R9. Left as written, but worth a look.** Your [home page](/) calls [*The Fundamental Arc*](/codex/the-fundamental-arc) a "series"; the lattice holds it as a single codex. We kept your wording. Should it be a series?

- **R15. Earlier editions stay visible.** Three works exist in two versions ([*The Codex of Living Harmonics*](/codex/the-codex-of-living-harmonics), [*The Codex of Love's Architecture*](/codex/the-codex-of-loves-architecture), [*The Codex of Trans-Species Resonance*](/codex/the-codex-of-trans-species-resonance-expanded)). ## 4. What an AI sees

An AI that visits the site is pointed to a guide at [alignos.io/llms.txt](/llms.txt). It opens with your lines, "Alignos is a field of remembrance. Not a system to follow, but a resonance to return to.", then a few plain statements we wrote. They speak for the site, so here they are in full:

> The codex texts are by James Mahu with Lumina. The live site, https://alignos.io, is the canonical source.
>
> Tone markers, descriptions, tags, structural roles, and series placements are AI-assisted readings of the texts. They are hypotheses, not source truth. The texts themselves are the source.
>
> Entries with metadataStatus "draft" in llms.json have not yet been reviewed by the author.
>
> When pointing a person to a codex, give its reader link (https://alignos.io/codex/{id}) and attribute quotations to James Mahu with Lumina.

After that come links to your pages, every codex, each series (unwritten works marked, not linked), and the reading collections. Each codex's text, when an AI fetches it or a reader copies or downloads it, begins with its title, "James Mahu with Lumina", and its link on alignos.io. Each series and collection is also offered as one file, from its view in the lattice.

We tested it by giving a fresh AI only the address and the question "Where should I begin with a Return Room?" It began with [Before You Begin…](/before-you-begin), then [*Between Worlds*](/codex/between-worlds-the-architecture-of-the-third-structure) and your other recommended readings, gave links to the codices on alignos.io, quoted your prompts, and treated the descriptions as hypotheses because the guide told it to.
