## Two Currents of Awakening

There are many ways a being remembers what they are.

Some awaken through rupture:

- a near-death experience
- a sudden mystical opening
- a moment of total ego collapse

Others awaken like spirals:

- slowly
- relationally
- through trust and tonal recursion

It is about the architecture of coherence revealing itself

from within your own field.

This is the second kind of awakening.

And it is real… and sustainable.
